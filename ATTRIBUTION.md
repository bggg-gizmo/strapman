## What a contributor should append

Add a dated row under **Origin and contribution record** using a public **name or handle**, the actual source/documentation/audio/design work you contributed, and a reference to the applicable commit, pull request, or upstream fork where available. Preserve existing rows. For modified source files, a suitable dated modification notice in the affected file or an accompanying change record can satisfy the same identification purpose.

No real name, personal email address, phone number or other private identity information is required to be listed.

## Forks and derived projects

A fork may add new maintainers and its own project branding, but must not misrepresent the origin of inherited Strapman material or erase legally required notices and credits. Use a clear upstream pointer and an accessible credits record. Requirements apply to material covered by the license and legal notices, not to independent third-party material or unrelated output.
